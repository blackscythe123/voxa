✎The way it flows

a small recipe in five steps, set in roman numerals like an old book.

  1. Sign in once, up top. cookies last weeks. most people do this on the first run and never again.
  2. Click into any text field, anywhere on the system. a chat box, a code editor, an email, the address bar. anywhere a cursor will rest.
  3. Press F9 to begin. a small paper strip pins itself to the corner. that's the timer.
  4. Speak naturally. no need to talk into the mic like a 1950s detective. just say what's on your mind.
  5. Press Escape or tap the strip. your words appear right where the cursor was, freshly transcribed and copied to the clipboard.
