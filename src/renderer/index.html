How to Use

  1. Click "Login to ChatGPT" above
  2. Complete your ChatGPT login in the browser that opens
  3. Click into any text field in any app
  4. Press Ctrl+Shift+R to start recording
  5. Speak naturally, then press Escape to stop
  6. Your transcript will be typed automatically
