How to Use

  1. Click "Open /api/auth/session"
  2. If you only see WARNING_BANNER, click "Open ChatGPT Login Page" and log in
  3. Open /api/auth/session again and copy the full JSON
  4. Paste JSON in the app and click "Use This Session Data"
  5. Click into any text field in any app
  6. Press Ctrl+Shift+R to start recording
  7. Speak naturally, then press Escape to stop
  8. Your transcript will be typed automatically

Recording widget appears in the corner when active
