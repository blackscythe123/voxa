Voxa v7.1: native screens, fixed and complete

v7 with every fix from docs/rebuild/03-ui-critique-v7.md, plus Insights and guided setup. One story runs through every screen: it is 10:45, the ChatGPT session ended at 10:40, a Gmail dictation from 10:42 is waiting, so every surface shows the same signed-out state. Android: one app screen with a gear for Settings, no tab bar; the Voxa bar lives in the keyboard's own toolbar. Windows: History is the home page, Settings and Insights beside it, the strip is a Fluent overlay with the Voxa mark and the target app. The ink line follows real speech: bursts and pauses.

Android · the keyboard
The Voxa mic lives in the keyboard's toolbar next to the suggestions, with a small dot for readiness (green ready, amber needs you). While you talk, the toolbar becomes the Voxa bar. Text goes in after you stop; nothing appears in the field while you talk.

Ready. Suggestions on the left, the Voxa mic on the right with a green dot.

Talking. Voxa mark, the live ink line, timer, Cancel and Stop, all in the toolbar row. The keys stay put.

Signed out, caught before you talk. The dot is amber; tapping the mic shows this instead of recording.

Android · every other keyboard state
Same toolbar row, same words as desktop (they come from the shared core). Each needs-you state has one action and a close.
Android · the app
One screen. History is home, anything waiting is pinned on top, Settings and Insights behind the top-bar icons. No bottom tab bar: Voxa has two destinations, not three.

Home is History. The waiting dictation offers Sign in, then Copy, because the Gmail draft is no longer in front.

Settings. The account says signed out here too, with a line on why Voxa uses ChatGPT.

Insights. Its own page: this week's words, time saved, streak, where you dictate.

Android · guided setup
Four steps, one thing each. It explains the ChatGPT sign-in before asking, tests the mic with the same ink line, turns on the keyboard, and ends with a real first try.
Windows · dictating
The strip is a Fluent overlay: acrylic, 8px corners, the Voxa mark, the app it will paste into, the live ink line, timer, a hint for the shortcut, standard Windows buttons. It sits in the gap above the taskbar and never takes focus.

Windows · the window
History is the home page. What needs you is on top as InfoBars (one per problem, each with one action and a close), then a one-line ready status, then today. Settings and Insights are their own pages; nothing is repeated between them.
