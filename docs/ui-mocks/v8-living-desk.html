Voxa, the living desk (v8)

Grown from today's recording strip instead of replacing it: the vellum slip, the terracotta pin, italic Fraunces, the typewriter timer and the quill that writes. Every state is a short note on the same slip, every surface moves, and the app itself is a writer's journal. Click the states below or let it play.

The slip, on your desk

Press Ctrl Space in WhatsApp. The slip pins itself above the taskbar, writes while you talk, scribbles while it writes it out, ticks when it lands. When it needs you, it says so in one line on the same slip.

First run: a letter, not a checklist

Four pages, one thing each. It explains the ChatGPT sign-in before asking, lets you watch the quill draw your voice, and ends with a real first dictation.
