Voxa rebuild, design directions (v4)

Three looks on one shared structure. The structure comes from the flow audit: the recording strip always shows what is happening and offers one action, and readiness (session, mic, network, paste) is checked before you talk. Pick a direction (or mix: e.g. A's strip with B's Hub). All three are buildable in GPUI.

Ink. Dark, quiet utility in the Raycast / Linear family. Geist type, one acid-lime accent used only for the primary action, so the eye always finds the one thing to do. Feels fast and pro; lowest visual noise on top of other apps.

Paper. Voxa's current warm identity, grown up. Fraunces serif display with Instrument Sans body, terracotta accent, cream surfaces. Distinctive and human, closest to Wispr Flow's warmth. Contrast fixed versus today (secondary text now 4.5:1 or better).

Signal. Bright and bold. Heavy Schibsted Grotesk, cobalt accent, generous radii, a black strip that pops over any app. Most confident and consumer-friendly; reads well at a glance on phones.

What changes versus today

  • Removed: the desktop's second settings window (preferences.html); the Android voice-only keyboard and the unregistered overlay service (one keyboard, mic always visible); settings that did nothing (Hold to talk, Trim silence, history row cap); the "captured silence" panel that showed even when nothing failed.
  • Kept: hotkey-to-paste on desktop, the HeliBoard keyboard base on Android, ChatGPT sign-in via an embedded page, History with audio replay.
  • Added: readiness check before recording; a strip that stays up with one action per failure; saved audio for every dictation with send-when-online; paste goes to the app you started in, with Undo and clipboard restore; Cancel; live mic level; language choice; Insert last / Undo on Android; a live setup checklist on both platforms.
  • Accessibility floor for every direction: text 4.5:1 or better, 12px/12sp minimum, every control keyboard-reachable and labelled, strip state announced to screen readers, reduced-motion respected.
